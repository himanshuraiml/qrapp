## Brand & Aesthetic
This design system represents **Academic QR Attendance**—a highly premium, accessible, and responsive tool designed for academic institutions.
The aesthetic is built on **Glassmorphism**, combining high translucency, micro-animations, and smooth organic shadows.

### Colors
* **Primary Blue (#2563EB):** Represents trust, logic, and academic precision. Used for primary CTAs and active states.
* **Secondary Indigo (#4F46E5):** Adds depth, luxury, and visual richness.
* **Muted Slate backgrounds:** Offers soft, low-fatigue dashboard foundations.

### Components & Elevation
* **Cards & Containers:** `bg-white/70 backdrop-blur-lg border border-white/40` with deep organic drop-shadows.
* **Scanning UI:** Deep dark elements with bright neon status outlines and smooth laser tracking effects.
